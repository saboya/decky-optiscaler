These headers MUST match the OptiScaler build shipped in bin/. The layout
mirror the ASI uses is generated from Config.h, so a mismatch means the ASI
reads the wrong offsets - it fails its validation checks and stays inert.

The files were moved into subfolders upstream after v0.9.4 and are flattened
here: hooks/FG_Hooks.cpp, menu/menu_common.cpp, upscalers/FeatureProvider_*.cpp
and wrapped/wrapped_swapchain.cpp. Enum types the Config class uses (FSR4Model,
FSR4Support, Upscaler, SharpenShader) are declared in OptiTypes.h, which is not
vendored; scripts/generate_asi_layout.py carries their underlying types.

unordered_dense.h is unchanged: the external/unordered_dense submodule is still
at 73f3cbb237e84d483afafc743f1f14ec53e12314 at this commit.

STATUS OF THE STATE MIRRORS in asi/live.cpp (state_head, state_fg_block,
state_tail and the changeBackend/newBackend search): they were written against
the v0.9.4 State.h and have NOT been ported to this commit. State::newBackend is
an Upscaler enum here rather than a std::string, FGchanged/SCchanged and the
FFX version lists moved, and DeviceAdapterNames is gone. Everything that
depends on them fails closed and reports itself unavailable. Config-based
controls (frame generation toggle) use the regenerated Config mirror.
